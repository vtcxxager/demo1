RESIDENTIAL · VIJAYAWADA

Modern 3BHK Residence

A full-home interior for a young family, built around one idea: every room should feel calm enough to come home to.

Client

Private residence

Location

Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh

Type

3BHK Apartment, 1,650 sqft

Scope

Full interior design & execution

Duration

11 weeks

The Brief

The clients had just bought their first apartment and wanted it to feel warm and settled from day one — not like a showroom. They asked for a layout that worked for a toddler now and would still make sense in ten years.

The Design

We planned the home around three zones: a quiet living area near the entrance, a family kitchen-dining space at the centre, and private bedrooms tucked toward the back. Sightlines were kept open between the living and dining areas so the space reads larger than its footprint.

Materials

Warm oak-toned laminate for storage units, honed beige marble-look flooring in shared areas, brushed brass hardware, and linen-textured upholstery that hides everyday wear.

The Transformation

The apartment came to us as bare shell — no false ceiling, no wardrobes, builder-grade flooring throughout. Every surface, fixture and piece of furniture in the finished home was specified and sourced by our team.

The Result

"They understood exactly what we wanted and turned our ideas into something much better than we imagined."

— Priya & Rahul
